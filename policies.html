Appointment Policies

A few kind boundaries keep every appointment comfortable, clean, and on schedule. Thank you for understanding.

  • I do not work over another nail artist’s work.
  • Please ask before bringing any additional guests. Numen Nails is home-based, so please respect the space.
  • Please do not book while sick. If you become sick before your appointment, reschedule as soon as possible.
  • A $10 deposit is required through Cash App. Deposits are nonrefundable if you cancel.
  • Please arrive on time. A five-minute grace period is provided because other clients may be scheduled afterward.
  • Appointments may take 2–3 hours. Numen Nails is currently a beginner nail technician, so please plan accordingly.
  • Remaining balances are cash only.
  • Cancellations or reschedules must be requested at least 24 hours in advance.
